Research thesis

Agentic for Embodied

My goal is to move embodied AI beyond one-shot policy inference toward systems that can decompose long-horizon tasks, invoke the right models and skills, inspect their own execution, recover from failure, and turn experience into reusable competence.

01

Agentic VLA Systems

Orchestrating foundation policies, skill routers, Code-as-Policy programs, robot primitives, and perception tools for long-horizon tasks.

02

Verified Physical Execution

Grounding decisions with confidence-aware perception, constrained controllers, post-action verification, and targeted recovery.

03

Self-Evolving Embodied Agents

Mining rollout experience into reusable skills and workflows, validating them in simulation, and refining them with real-robot feedback.

Projects

Lead project · Agentic VLA

GigaHarness

A project I lead on agentic embodied systems that orchestrate VLA policies, Code-as-Policy skills, robot primitives, and external tools. GigaHarness decomposes long-horizon tasks, routes skills from live execution state, verifies post-action outcomes, and recovers or replans after failure. Its Real2Sim2Real loop mines rollout traces into candidate skills and workflows, validates them in simulation, and refines them with real-robot feedback.

The clip shows repeated real-robot pick-and-place collection across numbered target regions. In the full GigaHarness loop, each run is followed by outcome assessment and data logging, then an automated reset initiates the next rollout. Random resets broaden initial-state coverage, while reference-image resets improve consistency and repeatability across collection rounds.
